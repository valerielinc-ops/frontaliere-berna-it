Responsabilità principali

  • Key Responsibilities:
  • Create and develop 3D models and designs for IKEA products, with a focus on styling.
  • Collaborate with designers, engineers, and other stakeholders to ensure that products meet the required standards.
  • Conduct research and analysis to stay up-to-date with the latest design trends and technologies.
  • Provide design support and guidance to other teams and departments as needed.
  • Participate in design reviews and feedback sessions.
  • Ensure that all designs are in line with IKEA's brand values and design principles.

Requisiti principali

  • Bachelor's or Master's degree in Industrial Design, Product Design, or a related field.
  • Proficient in 3D design software such as Autodesk Maya, Blender, or ZBrush.
  • Strong understanding of design principles, materials, and manufacturing processes.
  • Excellent communication and collaboration skills.
  • Ability to work independently and as part of a team.
  • Strong problem-solving and critical thinking skills.
  • Familiarity with IKEA's products and design language is a plus.
